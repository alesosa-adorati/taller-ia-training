# Hostile Questions and Evidence-Based Answers

## 1. Why not just hire fewer people?

Kestrel added 63 administrative employees versus 11 clinicians over two years (**Reported — B1**), but the case does not establish that automation is the cause of or solution to that growth. The discovery found repetitive work but also judgment-heavy exceptions. A pilot is therefore a way to test whether specific work can be reduced before making staffing conclusions.

## 2. Why do we need AI at all?

Some opportunities may not need AI. The discovery explicitly preserves a no-AI path and identifies process improvements such as better routing and knowledge management. AI is relevant only where extraction, retrieval or summarization materially changes the workflow.

## 3. Why trust AI with healthcare data?

Kestrel should not send PHI to an unapproved general-purpose AI service. **Reported — B6:** Sandra requires a BAA, risk assessment, defined data flow, minimum-necessary data, auditability and human review. Those are pilot gates, not optional controls.

## 4. What if the model hallucinates?

The case already contains an example of a hallucinated policy (**Reported — B3**). The proposed pilots therefore do not delegate final judgment or autonomous submission. Golden-set testing, human verification and production sampling are required.

## 5. How do you know prior authorization is the biggest problem?

The discovery does not claim it is the biggest problem across all administrative work. It has the strongest combination of direct shadowing, measurable volume, repetitive work and a separable human-judgment boundary among the workflows examined. Central scheduling, credentialing and full Revenue Cycle shadowing remain gaps.

## 6. Why not automate patient balance calls?

The case says the first 30 seconds are scripted but later conversations involve hardship, payment plans and distress (**Reported — B3**). That creates a different judgment and patient-impact profile. The discovery therefore treats autonomous calling as a no-go and leaves room for non-AI routing/process improvements.
