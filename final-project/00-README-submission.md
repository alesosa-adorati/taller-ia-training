# Kestrel Health Group — Final Discovery Project

## Engagement overview

This submission documents an end-to-end discovery engagement for Kestrel Health Group using Track B, the self-serve case.

**Measured — B1:** The in-scope population is 473 administrative employees across Revenue Cycle, Practice Operations, People/Talent and Patient Growth.

The engagement moves from client context and hypotheses through workflow discovery, maturity assessment, opportunity ranking, recommendation, pilot design and reflection.

## Evidence rule

Every substantive number is labeled as **Measured**, **Reported**, or **Projected**. Contradictions and unknowns are preserved rather than silently resolved.

## Deliverables

- `01-Admin/` — client brief, vocabulary and initial prediction
- `02-Stakeholders/` — stakeholder map and personas
- `04-Process-maps/` — workflow inventory, maps, baselines and verification
- `05-Opportunities/` — opportunity cards, backlog, matrix and cross-team pattern
- `06-Maturity/` — maturity scorecard
- `07-Readouts/` — final report, readout outline and hostile questions
- `08-Pilot/` — pilot specification, golden set and harness run
- `09-Reflection/` — decision log and AI use disclosure

## Coverage limitations

The case deliberately contains gaps: central scheduling was not covered by shadowing, Practice Operations had the lowest survey response rate, there was no RCM shadowing, credentialing was not shadowed, and no clinician was interviewed.

## Submission principle

This project is a discovery artifact, not a vendor recommendation. Recommendations are tied to observed or reported workflow evidence and explicitly identify where further validation is required.
