# Persona — COO / Executive Sponsor

**Representative:** Marcus Bell  
**Role:** COO  
**Type:** Executive sponsor / decision maker

## Goals

- Understand what the administrative workforce actually does.
- Determine which work requires a person.
- Reduce cost to collect.
- Avoid technology that does not reduce work.
- Identify a business owner for implementation.

**Reported — B1:** Marcus stated that the answer could be to hire fewer people and buy nothing.

**Reported — B3:** He identified finding an internal owner as a key problem.

## Decision criteria

The executive recommendation needs:

1. A measured or clearly labeled baseline.
2. A specific workflow change.
3. A distinction between human judgment and repeatable work.
4. A measurable pilot metric.
5. A named owner.
6. A governance and technical path.
7. A decision date.

## Business context

**Measured — B1:** Cost to collect is 4.1%.

**Measured — B1:** Days in A/R increased from 41 to 46.

**Measured — B1:** First-pass claim acceptance is below 88%.

These metrics establish the business context but do not prove causality for any individual workflow.

## Communication preference

The final readout should emphasize evidence, workflow change, baseline, projected impact, effort, owner and decision required rather than generic AI capabilities or vendor features.

## Evidence limitation

This persona is based on case statements rather than a direct interview.
