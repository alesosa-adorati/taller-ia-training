# AI Use Disclosure

## Where AI was used

AI assistance was used to organize the supplied Kestrel case evidence, draft discovery artifacts, structure workflow maps, create opportunity cards, prepare pilot evaluation material and identify evidence gaps.

## What AI was not allowed to do

The AI was not treated as an independent source of Kestrel facts. It did not invent measured baselines, fill unknowns with assumptions or resolve contradictions without labeling them.

## Verification performed

The project content was checked against the supplied B1–B9 case dossier. Numbers were labeled as measured, reported or projected/calculated where applicable. Unknowns and deliberate case gaps were retained.

## Important limitation

The final artifacts remain a simulated Track B discovery engagement. No real Kestrel interview, system access or production validation occurred.
