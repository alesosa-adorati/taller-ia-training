# Kestrel Discovery Vocabulary

| Term | Plain-language meaning | Why it matters | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net patient revenue | Revenue generated from patient care after applicable adjustments | Core business outcome | B1 |
| Cost to collect | Administrative cost associated with collecting patient and payer revenue | Primary COO priority | B1 |
| Days in A/R | Average days revenue remains in accounts receivable | Collection-cycle performance | B1 |
| First-pass claim acceptance | Percentage of claims accepted without first-submission rejection | Indicates upstream claim quality | B1 |
| Prior authorization | Payer approval required before certain services | Major Practice Operations workload | B3/B4 |
| Payer | Insurance organization responsible for eligible payment | Determines authorization and billing rules | B3 |
| Clearinghouse | Service exchanging claims between providers and payers | ClearBridge is Kestrel's clearinghouse | B1/B9 |
| Denial | Claim or payment request rejected by a payer | Creates downstream revenue-cycle work | B3/B9 |
| Appeal | Formal request for reconsideration of a denial | Requires documentation and policy evidence | B3 |
| PHI | Protected Health Information | Determines AI/data-handling requirements | B6 |
| BAA | Business Associate Agreement | Required for applicable PHI vendors | B6 |
| Credentialing | Establishing provider qualifications with payers and organizations | Enables billable care | B3 |
| Payer enrollment | Registering a provider with an individual payer | Delays can prevent billing | B3 |
| A/R | Accounts receivable; money owed to the organization | Key revenue-cycle metric | B1 |

## Vocabulary notes

The vocabulary intentionally favors terms that appear in the case and are needed to interpret workflow evidence. The removed CPT entry is not required to understand the initial client brief.
