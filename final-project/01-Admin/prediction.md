# Kestrel Health Group — Initial AI Maturity Prediction

These are hypotheses recorded before detailed workflow scoring.

| Dimension | Initial prediction | Reasoning |
|---|---:|---|
| Leadership / Strategy | 2.5 / 5 | Leadership recognizes the administrative-cost problem, but there is no clear enterprise AI strategy or named AI owner. |
| People / Skills | 2.0 / 5 | Employees experiment with AI, but training and sanctioned use are limited. |
| Data | 2.5 / 5 | Major systems contain structured data, but operational knowledge is fragmented across spreadsheets and people. |
| Tools / Technology | 2.5 / 5 | APIs exist, but portals, fax, spreadsheets and legacy databases remain important. |
| Governance / Risk | 1.5 / 5 | No administrative AI policy and no BAA with a general-purpose AI vendor. |
| Adoption / Usage | 2.0 / 5 | Experimentation exists, but adoption is inconsistent and much usage is outside sanctioned tooling. |

## Evidence behind prediction

**Reported — B1:** Marcus requested discovery and has a defined business priority.

**Measured — B2:** 51% of survey respondents reported using AI for work, but only 6% reported AI training.

**Measured — B2:** 71% of AI users reported using personal accounts.

**Reported — B6:** PHI use requires BAA/risk assessment and Sandra estimates a minimum 6–8 week path.

The final scorecard must test these hypotheses against workflow evidence, artifacts, system constraints and adoption behavior.
