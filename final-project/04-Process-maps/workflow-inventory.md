## Deliberate deep-dive selection

The three workflows selected for detailed process mapping are:

1. **WF-O1 Prior authorization** — strongest combination of observed work, measurable volume, fragmented information and explicit augmentation boundary.
2. **WF-O2 Referral intake** — directly observed, high monthly volume, substantial fax dependency and a clear human judgment boundary.
3. **WF-R3 Denial appeal preparation** — links downstream revenue-cycle work to recurring upstream causes and has evidence of existing reusable appeal knowledge.

The selection deliberately covers two departments and connects upstream operational work with downstream revenue-cycle consequences.

## Coverage limitations

Central scheduling was not observed. Revenue Cycle had no shadowing session. Credentialing was reported rather than shadowed. The referral keying error rate and existing-auth-not-attached rate remain unknown.
