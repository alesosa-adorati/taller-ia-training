# Baselines and Arithmetic

## WF-O1 — Prior authorization

**Measured — B9:** 2,400 requests/month.

**Measured — B9:** 61% require at least one chase.

Projected chased requests/month:

`2,400 × 61% = 1,464`

This is a **calculated volume from measured inputs**, not a directly observed count.

**Measured — B4:** Observed new-request examples: 19m, 14m and 17m.

**Measured — B4:** Observed chase examples ranged from approximately 3–21m.

No full-team average handling time is established.

## WF-O2 — Referral intake

**Measured — B9:** 5,800 referrals/month.

**Measured — B9:** 62% arrive by fax.

Projected fax referrals/month:

`5,800 × 62% = 3,596`

This is a calculated volume from measured inputs.

**Reported — B3:** Aaron estimates approximately 25% are problematic.

If used for planning, this remains a reported estimate and should not be converted into a measured error rate.

**Measured — B4:** Clean observed examples were approximately 4–7 minutes; problematic examples included 13 and 16 minutes.

No representative average handling time is established.

## WF-R3 — Appeals

**Measured — B9:** 3,100 denials are worked/month.

**Measured — B9:** 34% have missing/invalid PA.

Calculated monthly volume in this category:

`3,100 × 34% = 1,054`

This calculation applies the reported category percentage to the worked-denial volume and assumes the percentage is applicable to the same population.

**Measured — B9:** 58% of appeals are overturned.

**Reported — B3:** One appeal took 40+ minutes to prepare.

No representative average appeal handling time is established.

## Work time versus elapsed time

Prior authorization contains both active work and payer waiting. A 21-minute observed chase does not mean 21 minutes of continuous human work.

Credentialing has a reported 90–120 day cycle, but that is elapsed cycle time, not labor time.

## Contradictions / unknowns

- Existing authorization attachment rate is unknown.
- Referral keying error rate is unknown.
- Full average handling times are unknown for all three deep-dive workflows.
- Denial workflow was not shadowed.
- Some volume numbers are system volumes, while some task-time numbers are observations from small samples.
