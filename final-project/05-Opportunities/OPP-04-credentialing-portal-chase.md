# OPP-04 — Credentialing Status Consolidation

## Source workflow

WF-P1/WF-P2 — payer enrollment and recredentialing.

## Workflow change

Consolidate enrollment status and outstanding forms/documents into one operational view instead of manually checking payer sites.

## Impact

**Measured — B9:** 35 new provider payer enrollments/quarter and 60 recredentialing events/quarter.

**Reported — B3:** There are approximately 14 payer form/document sets per enrollment and 90–120 day chase cycles.

No observed labor-time baseline exists.

## Suitability

Repetitive and measurable, but sensitive data and many payer-specific sites make integration and governance material.

## Verdict

**INVESTIGATE / DEFER PILOT** until credentialing is shadowed and data-access requirements are validated.
