# OPP-05 — Practice Growth Reporting Assist

## Source workflow

WF-G1 — Monthly practice growth report.

## Workflow change

Automate report assembly and draft narrative while retaining human validation.

## Impact

**Reported — B3:** Ellis estimates 6 hours for assembly and 3 hours for narrative/deck work.

**Reported — B3:** Four Copilot licenses exist in the team.

## Suitability

The workflow is relatively low-risk and already uses Copilot, but its business impact is smaller than the core cost-to-collect workflows.

## Verdict

**NO-GO FOR PRIORITY PILOT** — not because the workflow is unsuitable, but because the current evidence does not connect it strongly enough to the engagement's primary business priority.
