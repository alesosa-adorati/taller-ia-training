# Opportunity 2x2 Matrix

## Axes

- **X-axis:** Evidence readiness — from low to high.
- **Y-axis:** Workflow change potential — from low to high.

| Opportunity | Evidence readiness | Workflow change potential | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPP-01 Prior Auth Chase | High | High | High / High |
| OPP-02 Referral Extraction | High | High | High / High |
| OPP-03 Appeal Evidence Assist | Medium | High | Medium / High |
| OPP-04 Credentialing | Medium | Medium | Medium / Medium |
| OPP-05 Growth Reporting | Medium | Low | Medium / Low |
| OPP-06 Patient Balance Autonomy | Medium | Low/unsafe | Medium / Low |

The matrix is intentionally not a financial ROI ranking. It is a discovery prioritization view based on evidence and workflow-change potential.
