## Scoring approach

Each opportunity was assessed against five suitability dimensions: repetitiveness, data availability, separability of human judgment, measurability and governance feasibility. Scores are qualitative because the case does not provide enough evidence for a defensible numeric ROI score across all opportunities.

## Key pattern

The backlog favors augmentation where repetitive information work can be separated from judgment. It avoids autonomous replacement where patient impact or clinical/financial judgment is central.
