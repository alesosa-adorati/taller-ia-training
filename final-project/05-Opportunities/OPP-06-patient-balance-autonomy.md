# OPP-06 — Autonomous Patient Balance Calling

## Source workflow

WF-R4 — Patient balance calls.

## Proposed change

Automate patient balance conversations.

## Evidence

**Reported — B3:** Ray described the first 30 seconds as highly scripted but later conversation as unscripted, including financial hardship, payment plans and distress.

## Suitability

The workflow contains significant human judgment and patient interaction.

## Verdict

**NO-GO** for autonomous AI calling in this discovery.

The evidence supports preserving human judgment rather than replacing it. A non-AI improvement such as better scripting, routing or escalation criteria could be explored separately.
